# PR reviewer

- **Who they are:** an independent reviewer — usually an agent — seeing the
  change for the first time.
- **What they already know:** the repo's conventions, the process doc, the
  surrounding code.
- **What they don't know:** the change itself, the constraints discovered
  while making it, what was already tried and abandoned.
- **Why they're reading:** to find what is wrong before it merges.
- **What they must leave with:** what changed and why, how it was verified,
  where the author thinks the risk concentrates, and what is deliberately out
  of scope.
- **What wastes their time:** restated diffs, unverifiable claims ("should
  work"), missing negative space — what was *not* done, unstated.
