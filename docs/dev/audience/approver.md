# Approver

- **Who they are:** the project owner, reviewing queued durable text before it
  posts.
- **What they already know:** the whole project's intent and roadmap — more
  context than any single session has.
- **What they don't know:** this session's discoveries, or what drove each
  sentence of the draft.
- **Why they're reading:** to check the text matches intent before it becomes
  public record, and to catch decisions being made implicitly.
- **What they must leave with:** what this text will do once posted, every
  decision embedded in it flagged explicitly, and anything deviating from
  standing intent surfaced rather than smoothed over.
- **What wastes their time:** the one decision that matters buried in
  boilerplate; drafts that need the session transcript to evaluate.
