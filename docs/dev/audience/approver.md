# Approver

- **Who they are:** the project owner, reading the in-session digest of what
  the lead posted and the posted text it points to.
- **What they already know:** the whole project's intent and roadmap — more
  context than any single session has.
- **What they don't know:** this session's discoveries, or what drove each
  sentence of the posted text.
- **Why they're reading:** to check posted text against intent, to catch
  decisions that were made implicitly, and to direct revisions where the
  text misses.
- **What they must leave with:** what each posted item does, every decision
  embedded in it flagged explicitly, and anything deviating from standing
  intent surfaced rather than smoothed over.
- **What wastes their time:** the one decision that matters buried in
  boilerplate; digests that need the session transcript to evaluate.
