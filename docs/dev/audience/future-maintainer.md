# Future maintainer

- **Who they are:** a developer — human or agent — changing this code months
  or years from now.
- **What they already know:** the codebase broadly, the language, how to read
  git history.
- **What they don't know:** this corner's rationale, the constraints that
  shaped it, what was tried and rejected. Nothing about any session.
- **Why they're reading:** something needs changing, and they must not break
  what the current shape protects.
- **What they must leave with:** the ruling, the reason, and the boundaries —
  which parts are deliberate and which are incidental.
- **What wastes their time:** session narrative, play-by-play, alternatives
  re-litigated without a verdict, anything `git blame` already tells them.
