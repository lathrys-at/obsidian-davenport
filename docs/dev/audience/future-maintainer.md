# Future maintainer

- **Who they are:** The future maintainer is a developer, human or agent. The
  future maintainer changes this code months or years from now.
- **What they already know:** The future maintainer knows the codebase in
  general terms. The future maintainer knows the language. The future
  maintainer knows how to read git history.
- **What they don't know:** The future maintainer does not know the rationale
  for this corner of the code. The future maintainer does not know the
  constraints that shaped this corner. The future maintainer does not know
  what was tried and rejected. The future maintainer knows nothing about any
  session.
- **Why they're reading:** Something must change. The future maintainer must
  not break what the current shape protects.
- **What they must leave with:** The future maintainer must leave with the
  ruling, the reason, and the boundaries. The boundaries identify which parts
  are deliberate and which parts are incidental.
- **What wastes their time:** The session narrative wastes the future
  maintainer's time. A play-by-play account wastes the future maintainer's
  time. Alternatives argued again with no ruling waste the future
  maintainer's time. Anything that `git blame` already tells the future
  maintainer wastes the future maintainer's time.
